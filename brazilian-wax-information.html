Brazilian wax information

Everything worth knowing before your first one, from what actually happens to how the pricing works.

What you can expect

A Brazilian removes everything from the labia, including between the bottom cheeks if required. The front is yours to style, whether that is a strip, a triangle, or taken all off. If you would rather keep more, have a look at the bikini options on the waxing menu.

Most Brazilians are done in 15 to 30 minutes, including the chat at the start. We wax all day, every day, and being quick is exactly what makes it bearable. Your therapist will talk you through what is happening as they go. No lectures, no upselling.

Hygiene is of paramount importance here, so you can rest assured that you are in safe, capable hands.

Which wax?

Hot wax is applied warm and lifted away without a strip, so it grips the hair rather than your skin. It is the pick if you are new to waxing, sensitive, or pregnant.

Strip wax is quicker across larger areas and great for regulars. A full strip wax braz is not available to clients new to Brazilian waxing, as it can be a little tough on sensitive areas.

Not sure? Say so when you arrive and your therapist will pick for you.

How the pricing works

Your first Brazilian at Strip makes you a Strip Club member automatically. After that, our booking system works out your price from the number of weeks since you were last in. Come back sooner and you pay less, because there is less hair and it is finer, which makes the appointment quicker for everyone.

Because the price moves with your own visits, the booking system is the only place it is ever up to date. Open it and your current price shows before you confirm.

Your questions

How long does my hair need to be?

About a quarter of an inch, roughly the size of a grain of rice. Much shorter and the wax cannot grip it. Much longer and we may trim it first so the wax can do its job. Please do not shave in between appointments.

Will it hurt?

There is a sting, and we will not pretend otherwise. But we aim to have you done in 15 to 30 minutes rather than 35 to 60, and a fast, confident technique with skin conditioning wax is far more comfortable than most people expect. It gets easier every visit.

Why is there hair left after my first wax?

If it is your first wax, or it has been more than eight weeks, your hair will not all be on the same growth cycle, so a little can come through in the days after. It evens out once you are waxing regularly, usually every four to six weeks.

Can I be waxed on my period, or while pregnant?

Both are fine. On your period, just wear a tampon or cup and let us know. Skin can be more sensitive at that time of the month and during pregnancy, so we would recommend hot wax rather than strip wax.
