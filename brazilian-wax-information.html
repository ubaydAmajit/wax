Brazilian wax information

Everything worth knowing before your first Brazilian — the waxes we use, what it costs, and how to get the best result.

Two ways to do it

Full hot wax

High-quality vanilla hot wax throughout. It sets on the hair and lifts away without a strip, so it pulls at the hair rather than the skin.

Best for: sensitive skin, first-timers, and anyone who is pregnant.

Lycon strip wax across the front where it’s quicker, hot wax on the more sensitive areas. The fastest option, and our most popular.

Best for: regulars and anyone who wants to be in and out.

Common questions

How long does my hair need to be?

About a quarter of an inch — roughly the length of a grain of rice. Much shorter and the wax can’t grip it; much longer and we may trim it first so the wax works properly.

How long does the appointment take?

Most Brazilians are done in 15 to 30 minutes, including the chat at the start. We wax all day, every day — speed is the whole point.

Will it hurt?

There’s a sting, and we won’t pretend otherwise. But a quick, confident technique and skin-conditioning wax make it far more comfortable than most people expect, and it gets easier with every visit.

What should I do beforehand?

Gently exfoliate a couple of days before, skip heavy moisturiser or oil on the day, and don’t shave in between appointments — shaving blunts the hair and brings on ingrowns. Loose cotton clothing afterwards is a good idea.

Why is there a little hair left after my first wax?

If it’s your first wax, or it’s been more than eight weeks, your hair won’t all be on the same growth cycle, so some can come through shortly after. That evens out once you’re on a regular rhythm — usually every four to six weeks.

What about afterwards?

Your skin may feel warm or look a little pink for a few hours — that’s normal. Skip hot baths, saunas, swimming pools and the gym for the first 24 hours, and start exfoliating gently again after a couple of days to keep ingrowns away.

Can I get waxed on my period?

Yes — just wear a tampon or cup and let us know. Skin can be a bit more sensitive around that time, so some people prefer to book a few days either side.

Still have a question?

Ask us before you book — we’d rather you turned up knowing exactly what to expect.
