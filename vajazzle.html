Vajazzles at Strip range in price from $5 to $30 and can last up to 7 days. Add a vajazzle on to your Brazilian wax and prepare to shine!

Or, as those good folk at Wikipedia put it:

A vajazzle is a form of genital decoration, formed by the application of crystal ornaments to the shaved pubic area of a woman. The process is known as vajazzling. The phenomenon was popularized by singer Jennifer Love Hewitt, who devoted a chapter in her book The Day I Shot Cupid to vajazzling. During a promotional interview on a US talk show in January 2010 she advised her audience to vajazzle their vajayjays. Vajazzle became the most searched term on Google the following day.
