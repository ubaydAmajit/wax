Hot wax or strip wax?

Hot wax is applied warm and lifts off without a strip. It grips the hair rather than the skin, so it’s the pick for sensitive areas, first-timers, and anyone who’s pregnant.

Strip wax is faster over larger areas like legs, arms and backs, and costs a little less. Not sure? Tell your therapist and they’ll recommend one.

Good to know

Students with a current NZ student ID get 10% off — just show it at your appointment.

Hair needs to be around a quarter of an inch long, about the length of a grain of rice, for the wax to grip properly. Don’t shave in between visits.
