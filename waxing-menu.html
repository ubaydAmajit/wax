Hot wax or strip wax?

Hot wax is recommended for sensitive areas, as it is gentle on skin and will not pull it too much. We use a selection of Lycon's Pinkini and Vanilla Hot Wax, and you can add it on to any Brazilian or bikini service.

Strip wax is quicker over larger areas and a great option for our regular clients and nearly all bikini services. Please note that strip wax for Brazilians is only available to those who are used to the method, as it can be a little tough on sensitive areas. For new clients to Brazilian waxing, a full strip wax braz is not available.
