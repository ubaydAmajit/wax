Brazilian & bikini wax styles

Not sure what style you want? Here’s what each one actually removes.

Brazilian

Everything removed from the labia, including between the bottom cheeks if required. The front pubic area can be styled in a strip, a triangle, or taken all off.

Extended bikini

Hair removed from the sides and top, leaving a G-string style shape behind. Between the bottom cheeks can be added on.

Standard bikini

Hair removed from the sides and top, leaving a larger bikini style shape behind. Between the bottom cheeks can be added on.

Option one

Hot wax

Recommended for sensitive areas, as it’s gentle on skin and won’t pull it too much. We use a selection of Lycon’s Pinkini and Vanilla Hot Wax.

You can add hot wax on to any Brazilian or bikini wax service at Strip. It’s the pick if you’re new to waxing, sensitive, or pregnant.

Option two

Strip wax

Faster over larger areas, and a great option for our regular clients and nearly all bikini services.

Please note: strip wax for Brazilians is only available to those who are used to the method, as it can be a little tough on sensitive areas. For new clients to Brazilian waxing, a full strip wax Brazilian is not available.

Loyalty, without the card

Strip Club membership

The sooner you come back, the less you pay. That’s the whole programme.

Strip is pleased to offer our clients a fantastic loyalty program. Once you’ve had a Brazilian wax at Strip for the first time, you automatically become a Strip Club member and enjoy the savings and the smoothness.

Unlike other loyalty programs, you won’t be issued with a card. Our booking system records every visit you make, and your Brazilian wax price is calculated on the frequency of your visits to us.

It’s simple: once you’ve visited us and had your first Brazilian wax at Strip, you’ll be charged prices based on the number of weeks since your last appointment with us.

Please note, our prices are based on your last visit to Strip, not elsewhere.
