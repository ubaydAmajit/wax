About Strip

We only do waxing

Strip Wax Boutique is Wellington’s Brazilian wax specialist — a small studio that deliberately does one thing rather than a bit of everything.

We offer a full range of professional hair removal treatments from top to toe, with a particular specialty in Brazilian waxing, plus a range of male waxing treatments from chest to back.

We employ only Senior Waxing Experts, yet keep our prices set to give you the best value-for-money waxing in town. Treatments are fast and comparatively less painful — we aim to have you done in 15 to 30 minutes.

We use a range of top-quality skin-conditioning waxes for a more comfortable experience, and hygiene is of paramount importance here, so you can rest assured you’re in safe, capable hands.

Getting here

Finding the door

The James Smith Building has a few entrances — this is the one you want.

  1. Enter from street level

    Use the main doors between Stirling Sports and Platypus Shoes.

  2. Head up to level 1

    Take the stairs next to Rebel Sport.

  3. Follow the signs to Floatwell

    We’re just before Floatwell, near the elevators. Look for the Strip sign.
