Waxing FAQs

A little waxing education. When not to wax, how to prepare, how to look after your skin afterwards, and the questions we get asked most.

When not to wax

Waxing is not suitable in every situation. Please tell us when you book if any of these apply to you. It is usually not a problem, we just need to know.

Do not wax if you

  • Are using Roaccutane or another oral acne treatment, or have in the last 12 months
  • Use Retin-A, Differin, retinol or another topical exfoliating treatment on the area
  • Have had a chemical peel, laser treatment or microdermabrasion on the area recently
  • Are sunburnt, or the skin is broken, irritated or inflamed
  • Have a rash, cold sore, open cut, wart, mole or skin lesion in the area
  • Are taking antibiotics, blood thinning or steroid medication, without checking with your doctor first
  • Have had a recent cosmetic or surgical procedure on the area

If you are unsure, email us before you book. We would much rather talk it through than turn you away on the day.

Before waxing

Do

  • Let the hair grow to about a quarter of an inch, roughly the size of a grain of rice
  • Gently exfoliate the area two or three days beforehand
  • Shower before your appointment
  • Wear loose, breathable cotton clothing
  • Tell your therapist about any medication, skin condition or pregnancy
  • Take a paracetamol half an hour before if you are nervous about your first one

Don't

  • Shave between appointments, as it blunts the hair and brings on ingrowns
  • Apply moisturiser, oil or fake tan to the area on the day
  • Sunbathe or use a sunbed in the 24 hours before
  • Book straight after a hot spa, sauna or swim
  • Turn up with hair shorter than a grain of rice, as the wax cannot grip it

After waxing

Your skin may feel warm, tight or look a little pink for a few hours. That is completely normal and settles quickly.

For the first 24 hours, avoid

  • Hot baths, spas and saunas
  • Swimming pools and the beach
  • The gym, or anything that makes you sweat
  • Sunbeds and direct sun on the area
  • Fake tan, perfumed products and heavy moisturiser
  • Tight clothing and synthetic underwear

From day two

  • Start exfoliating gently again to keep ingrowns away
  • Moisturise daily with something unperfumed
  • Stick to loose cotton underwear where you can
  • Rebook every 4 to 6 weeks to keep your hair on one growth cycle

Your questions

What is a Brazilian?

Everything removed from the labia, including between the bottom cheeks if required, with the front pubic area styled in a strip, a triangle, or taken all off. There is a full breakdown on the Brazilian wax information page.

Is waxing popular with guys?

Very. Back, chest, shoulders and neck are our most booked men's services, and a full back is usually done in about 20 minutes. From chest to back, we have your manscaping options covered! Everything is on the waxing menu.

What should I expect after my first wax treatment?

Warm, slightly tight skin and some pinkness for a few hours. If it is your first wax, or it has been over eight weeks, your hair will not all be on the same growth cycle, so a little can come through in the days after. That evens out once you are waxing regularly.

Why choose waxing over shaving?

Waxing removes the hair from the root rather than cutting it at the surface, so you stay smooth for weeks rather than days. Regrowth comes back finer and softer, there is no stubble, and you avoid the itchiness and razor rash that comes with shaving. Wax regularly and there is less hair each time.

How do I avoid ingrown hairs?

Exfoliate gently a couple of times a week from day two, moisturise daily, wear loose cotton, and do not shave between appointments. Shaving is the single biggest cause of ingrowns in people who otherwise wax.

How often should I come back?

Every four to six weeks. That keeps your hair on one growth cycle, which means a cleaner result, a quicker appointment, and a lower price through Strip Club.

Can I be waxed on my period, or while pregnant?

Both are fine. On your period, just wear a tampon or cup and let us know. Skin can be more sensitive at that time of the month and during pregnancy, so we would recommend hot wax rather than strip wax.

What do you use?

A selection of Lycon's Pinkini and Vanilla Hot Wax, plus Lycon strip wax. Disposable gloves, spatulas and strips every time. We NEVER double dip, and hospital grade sterilisation is used for all non-disposables.

Where do I find current prices?

In the booking system. Brazilian pricing moves with your own visit history, so the booking calendar is the only place it is always right. Check your price.
