Waxing FAQs

Important

When not to wax

Waxing isn’t suitable in every situation. Please tell us at booking if any of these apply to you — it’s not a problem, we just need to know.

Please don’t book a wax if you

  • Are using Roaccutane, Accutane or another oral acne treatment, or have in the last 12 months
  • Use Retin-A, Differin, retinol or another topical exfoliating treatment on the area
  • Have had a chemical peel, laser treatment or microdermabrasion on the area recently
  • Are sunburnt, or have broken, irritated or inflamed skin in the area
  • Have a rash, cold sore, open cut, wart, mole or skin lesion in the area
  • Are taking antibiotics, or blood-thinning or steroid medication, without checking with your doctor first
  • Have had a recent cosmetic or surgical procedure on the area

If you’re unsure, email us before you book — we’d rather talk it through than turn you away on the day.

Preparation

Before your wax

Do

  • Let the hair grow to about a quarter of an inch — roughly a grain of rice
  • Gently exfoliate the area 48–72 hours beforehand
  • Shower before your appointment
  • Wear loose, breathable cotton clothing
  • Tell your therapist about any medication, skin condition or pregnancy
  • Take a paracetamol half an hour before if you’re nervous about your first one

Don’t

  • Shave between appointments — it blunts the hair and brings on ingrowns
  • Apply moisturiser, oil or fake tan to the area on the day
  • Sunbathe or use a sunbed in the 24 hours before
  • Book straight after a hot spa, sauna or swim
  • Turn up with hair shorter than a grain of rice — the wax can’t grip it

Aftercare

After your wax

Your skin may feel warm, tight or look a little pink for a few hours. That’s completely normal and settles quickly.

For the first 24 hours

  • No hot baths, spas or saunas
  • No swimming pools or the beach
  • No gym or anything that makes you sweat
  • No sunbeds or direct sun on the area
  • No fake tan, perfumed products or heavy moisturiser
  • No tight clothing or synthetic underwear

From day two onward

  • Start exfoliating gently again to keep ingrowns away
  • Moisturise daily with something unperfumed
  • Stick to loose cotton underwear where you can
  • Rebook every 4–6 weeks to keep the hair on one growth cycle

Your questions

What is a Brazilian?

Everything removed from the labia, including between the bottom cheeks if required, with the front pubic area styled as a strip, a triangle, or taken all off. There’s a full breakdown on the Brazilian wax information page.

Is waxing popular with guys?

Very. Back, chest, shoulders and neck are our most-booked men’s services, and a full back is usually done in about twenty minutes. Everything is on the waxing menu.

What should I expect after my first wax treatment?

Warm, slightly tight skin and some pinkness for a few hours. If it’s your first wax, or it’s been over eight weeks, your hair won’t all be on the same growth cycle, so a little can come through in the days after. That evens out once you’re waxing regularly.

How do I avoid ingrown hairs?

Exfoliate gently a couple of times a week from day two, moisturise daily, wear loose cotton, and don’t shave between appointments. Shaving is the single biggest cause of ingrowns in people who otherwise wax.

How often should I come back?

Every four to six weeks. That keeps your hair on one growth cycle, which means a cleaner result, a quicker appointment and — through Strip Club — a lower price.

Can I be waxed while pregnant?

Yes, and plenty of our clients are. Skin is often more sensitive during pregnancy, so we’d recommend hot wax rather than strip wax. Just let us know when you book.

What do you use?

A selection of Lycon’s Pinkini and Vanilla Hot Wax, plus Lycon strip wax. Disposable gloves, spatulas and strips every time — we never double-dip — and hospital grade sterilisation for everything non-disposable.

Where do I find current prices?

In the booking system. Brazilian pricing moves with your own visit history, so the booking calendar is the only place it’s always right. Check your price.
