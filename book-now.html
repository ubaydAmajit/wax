Cancellations & amendments

No show is a no no! Please request any amendment or cancellation at least 24 hours before your appointment. Email strip.wax.boutique@gmail.com or text 0210 275 5284.

Please be on time!

If you are late, your appointment will likely be cancelled and you will be charged 50% of the total booking price.

It is your responsibility to arrive with plenty of time to make your appointment. We cannot be held responsible for traffic, transport or parking hold ups, any other circumstance that delays you, or for not reading our location page and getting lost.

Payment

Eftpos and card payments are not available. Please pay by bank transfer at the studio.

Deposits

A deposit is required for some appointments. We will email you payment instructions after your appointment request.

Deposits are fully refundable if you wish to cancel or change your booking, providing you give at least 24 hours notice. We will either return the payment to you or hold it on your account for future use, whichever you prefer.

Why we have this policy

We have reluctantly implemented this policy, as no shows, late comers and cancellations within 24 hours have a big impact on us and on our ability to offer great prices. They are also not fair on others who could have had the space. Thank you for your understanding.
