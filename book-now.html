Before you arrive

Three quick things

01

Let the hair grow

About a quarter of an inch — roughly a grain of rice. Don’t shave in between appointments.

02

Bring your student ID

A current NZ student ID card gets you 10% off your treatment.

03

Allow 15–30 minutes

That’s the whole appointment. Give yourself a few extra minutes to find us on level 1.

Cancellations

Plans change — we just ask for as much notice as you can give, so we can offer the spot to someone else.
